## Key determinations enabled

1. **N.J.A.C. 16:32 currentness resolved.** Chapter 32 was readopted as R.2023 d.002 effective 2022-11-30 and now expires 2029-11-30 (chapter expiration note, p. 32-1). The prior archived courtesy copy's 2022-07-02 expiration is superseded. rule_021–026 are in current force as text-verified.
2. **Network designations resolved from the primary instrument itself** (16:32-1.3, -1.4, Appendices A–C): NJ 81 is National Network for its full length (MP 0.00 at I-95 to US 1 at ≈1.00, Elizabeth, Union Co.); Interstates (incl. I-95/NJ Turnpike, entire length) are National Network; US 1&9 in Elizabeth/Newark is a State highway not excepted in Appendix A → New Jersey Access Network; Appendix B excludes CR 514 between US 1&9 in Rahway and Bay Ave in Elizabeth (not on the modeled corridor); Appendix C contains no Union County 600-series entries.
3. **PA-10 currentness resolved**: the live tariff is byte-identical to the archived 2026-05-01 edition (SHA-256 match, 2026-07-16).
4. **Subrule 34-1140(c) history**: the clause "Any Drayage Truck that is not an Alternative (Drayage) Truck as defined in SUBRULE 34-1060" appears in every archived edition from June 2019 (deadline 2020-08-01, "1997 or older") through the current edition (deadline 2023-07-02, "1998 or older"; future stanzas 2027-05-01/"1999 or older" and 2028-05-01/"2000 or older"), always annotated "ISSUED 15 OCTOBER 2010". No PANYNJ erratum, amendment notice, or clarification addressing clause (c) was found on panynj.gov or in the FMC-schedule listings as of 2026-07-16.
5. **Elizabeth municipal layer resolved for the corridor**: North Avenue, North Avenue East, McLester Street, Tripoli Street, Kapkowski Road and Dowd Avenue do **not** appear in the 4-ton (10.16.010) or 5-ton (10.16.040) exclusion schedules; 10.16.050 (Ord. 4654, 2015-12-22) creates a **permissive** 80,000-lb overweight-vehicle zone on North Avenue East (from 440 ft east of Kapkowski Rd, 520 ft east, connecting to the Port Authority overweight vehicle zone); Ch. 10.40 restricts truck **parking** only (distinct act); movement chapters impose only traffic-control devices (signal at North Ave E/Kapkowski; stop signs on Dowd Ave).
6. **Roadway jurisdiction resolved by NJDOT LRS**: all modeled corridor streets carry municipal SRIs (subtype 7, prefix 2004 = Elizabeth, Union County). Residual ambiguity: ramp SLD names at the NJ 81 interchange reference "CR 624 NORTH AVE", and OSM tags North Avenue East with SRI 20000624, while the LRS "UNION COUNTY 624" SRI (subtype 6) maps 0.6 mi in the Westfield area; the county-vs-municipal designation exactly at the interchange is not fully resolved. Both branches yield "no verified restriction found" for the corridor.
