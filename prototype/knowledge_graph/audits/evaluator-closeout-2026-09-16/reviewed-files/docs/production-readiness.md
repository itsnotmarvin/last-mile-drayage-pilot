# Production release requirements

Status: blocked for production. Internal engineering plan, updated September 16, 2026.
The user requires production readiness before a stakeholder presentation.
No letter grade or certification is assigned by this document.

The supported first release must be defined before assessing completeness.
APM Elizabeth and the six study families are a development starting point;
they do not establish coverage of all New Jersey movements or every legal duty.
Every required gate must pass for the promised scope. Strong performance in one
area cannot offset a critical failure elsewhere.

Original-statement capture, review and controlled results now have a local
application implementation. The next release requirements are client acceptance,
reviewed evaluators and accepted verification evidence for a bounded first scope,
plus production identity and operations before any real-user deployment. Independent domain/security
review and operational validation are engineering acceptance activities; they
are separate from a stakeholder presentation.

## September 12 input-review increment

The [input-review implementation](input-review.md) adds write-time rejection of
recognized contradictions, defensive reassessment of historical saved facts,
explicit unconfirmed-extraction metadata, and a deterministic report. It does
not close the original-transcript, authenticated confirmation, application UI,
broader evaluator, or operational deployment gates.

No additional Claude experiment is running. The user's earlier preference to
pause wording experiments remains in force. Before production approval, however,
the actual released client/model workflow still needs independent acceptance
evidence. Backend tests cannot stand in for that result.

Zero observed critical failures in a test suite is necessary evidence to assess;
it does not prove a zero failure rate. A release decision needs documented scope,
reviewers, acceptance criteria, measured results, unresolved risks, and accountable
operational ownership. Authority approval, source rights, and real integrations
cannot be replaced by development assertions.
