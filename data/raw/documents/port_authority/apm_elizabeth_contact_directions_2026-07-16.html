Directions To Locations Cta

From Manhattan:
Both the Holland Tunnel and Lincoln Tunnel will take you to the NJ Turnpike South. Follow NJ Turnpike South directions below. 

Travelling south on Route 1 & 9: 
Follow Route 1 & 9 South until sign indicating Elizabeth Seaport/North Avenue exit.
Take this exit and follow North Avenue East to McLester Street.
Turn left on McLester Street.
Turn right at Tripoli Street (3rd light).
The entrance to the APM Terminals Control Building, Garage, Warehouse and Marine Operations will be on the right.

Travelling south on the NJ Turnpike: 
Take the NJ Turnpike to Exit 13A.
Follow the signs to Elizabeth Seaport/North Avenue.
Follow North Avenue to McLester Street.
Turn left on McLester Street. 
Turm right at Tripoli Street (3rd light).
The entrance to the APM Terminals Control Building, Garage, Warehouse and Marine Operations will be on the right.

Firm code: E425

What is this?
A unique identifier assigned by US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and used for import and export customs documentation, automated manifest system (AMS) filings and freight forwarding and cargo tracking systems.

Include this code on shipping instructions and in freight documentation as needed.
